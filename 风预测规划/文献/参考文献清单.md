## 已重点引用的文献

1. Xie et al., 2021. A Multivariable Data-Driven Prediction Model for Short-Term Wind Speed Forecasting. Atmosphere.
   链接：https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4433/12/5/651
   可借鉴点：多变量 LSTM、相关性筛选、短期风速预测基线。

2. Fuentes-Barrios et al., 2022. Short-Term Wind Speed Forecasting Using a LSTM Neural Network and WRF Model. Engineering Proceedings.
   链接：https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4931/19/1/30
   可借鉴点：10 分钟数据、过去 12 步预测未来 12 步、多步 nowcasting。

3. Sari et al., 2021. Deep convolutional long short-term memory network for forecasting wind speed and direction.
   链接：https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/18824889.2021.1894878
   可借鉴点：风速与风向联合预测、风向矢量化表达、CNN-LSTM 类模型。

4. Blazakis et al., 2025. ML-Based Multi-Horizon Wind Speed and Wind Direction Forecasting for Aviation and Energy Applications in Coastal Crete. Electronics.
   链接：https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/24/4856
   可借鉴点：多时域风速/风向预测、TFT/LightGBM/CatBoost/CNN 对比、不同预测长度下模型差异。

5. Mode et al., 2025. Short-term extreme wind speed forecasting using dual-output LSTM-based regression and classification model. Journal of Wind Engineering and Industrial Aerodynamics.
   链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167610525000315
   可借鉴点：极端风速预测、回归与分类双输出、事件识别评价。

6. Wang et al., 2025. Enhancing wind power forecasting accuracy through LSTM with adaptive wind speed calibration. Scientific Reports.
   链接：https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-89398-y
   可借鉴点：历史实测风与外部预报风校正融合。

7. Rajaperumal & Columbus, 2025. Wind power prediction using CNN-LSTM based on time series analysis. Scientific Reports.
   链接：https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-05250-3
   可借鉴点：CNN-LSTM 时序预测结构。

8. Feng et al., 2025. Probabilistic wind speed forecasting using multi-resolution historical data. Engineering Structures.
   链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0141029624016602
   可借鉴点：多分辨率历史数据、分位数回归、概率预测。

9. Zuege et al., 2025. Split conformal prediction for wind speed forecasting. International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems.
   链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142061525002510
   可借鉴点：风速预测不确定性量化、预测区间。

10. Gao et al., 2023. Dynamic adaptive spatio-temporal graph network for offshore wind speed forecasting. Applied Soft Computing.
    链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1568494623003125
    可借鉴点：多站点/空间相关风速预测；当前阶段只作后续扩展参考。

11. Liu & Chen, 2019. Data processing strategies in wind energy forecasting models and applications: A comprehensive review. Applied Energy.
    链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306261919308517
    可借鉴点：风预测数据预处理策略综述。

12. Stop unrealistic data preprocessing in machine learning based wind speed forecasting, 2026. Applied Mathematical Modelling.
    链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0307904X25004500
    可借鉴点：警惕全样本分解、全样本归一化等造成未来信息泄漏的处理方式。
